Case study · self-initiated demo

Wildberries product cards from one spreadsheet

Three card templates filled from a product table: main, reasons to buy, what's in the box. 18 cards in 7 seconds, every card checked automatically.

Self-initiated demo - not client work. The door shop is invented and the door illustrations are drawn in code.

The case

Task. A shop with hundreds of models needs three marketplace cards per model in one consistent style. Making them by hand in a design editor takes weeks and lets wrong numbers slip through.

Solution. Three templates (main image with key features, reasons to buy, contents and specifications) filled from the product spreadsheet and the client's photos. Long text shrinks to fit; if it still does not fit, the card is flagged instead of being cut off.

Delivered. 6 models × 3 templates = 18 cards at 900×1200, built in 7 seconds. Every card is checked for empty fields, missing photos, overflowing text, size and file weight, and all cards appear on one overview sheet for a quick visual check. No logo on the cards, following Wildberries rules.

Concept work for a fictional door shop; the door illustrations are drawn in code. The first stage for a real shop is three templates in its style and 10 sample cards.
