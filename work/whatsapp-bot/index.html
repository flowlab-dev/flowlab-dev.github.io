WhatsApp order bot, the same engine as on Telegram

The Telegram order bot now answers on WhatsApp too: one engine, one order database, buttons and lists, signed webhooks. 69 automated checks pass.

Self-initiated demo - not client work. The bakery is invented and the demo has no live WhatsApp number.

The case

Task. A bakery's customers write on WhatsApp, while its order bot already runs on Telegram. Two separate bots with two order lists would be hard to keep in step.

Solution. The same order bot now answers on WhatsApp as well, with one engine, one set of rules and one order database. Up to three choices show as buttons, longer ones as a list, and with more than ten options the customer can reply with a number. A customer who writes "Hi" gets the menu, and a real question goes to a person. Every message from Meta is checked by its signature, a repeated delivery never gets a double reply, and photos or voice notes do not break the order.

A whole order runs in an automated test against a fake WhatsApp API, with every message checked against WhatsApp's limits, and all 69 checks pass. There is no live number in the demo, and the bakery is invented. On a real project the WhatsApp Business account and number stay yours and I connect the bot through your access.
