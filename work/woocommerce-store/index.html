The case

Task. A small tea shop (invented for this demo) needs its own online store: a catalogue, product variants, delivery and payment, and the owner should hear about every order right away.

Solution. A WordPress and WooCommerce store with a custom theme instead of a bought template. The catalogue has categories, and one product comes in 50 g and 100 g, each with its own price and stock. Sale prices work too. Delivery follows the shop's rules: free from $50, and then the paid option is hidden so no one pays $6 by mistake; under $50 it's a flat $6. Customers pay on delivery or by bank transfer. The shop and the other pages share one style and are easy to use on a phone. The whole store (settings, catalogue, delivery, payments, pages, menus) is set up by one script, so it can be rebuilt from scratch.

Result. I placed a test order through the real checkout. It went through, the customer and the owner each got an e-mail in the brand colours, and stock went down by itself. Send me a link to a store you like and I'll tell you how long yours would take.
