The case

An internal tool behind the shop's website: every request lands on one board, from quote to shipping. The office sends prices, releases work and assigns people; the floor moves jobs through its own operations and never sees prices. Each part follows its own route, late work is said in words ("Late by 3 working days"), pauses carry a reason, and the week fits in one sentence. Search, filters, list view and CSV export.

57 automated checks. In this demo the data lives only in your browser; in a real project roles are protected by the server, not by buttons.
