The case

Task. A seed-stage startup that sells soil sensors to vineyards needs a deck investors remember after ten others: problem, product, market, business model, competitors, go-to-market, roadmap and the ask.

Solution. The whole deck is built on one picture: a vineyard map where every vine is a dot coloured by soil moisture, from dry clay to water. The same grid shows the problem (one valve for 24 vines), the product screen and the market. Market circles are drawn at their true area ratio, 1.1M : 160k : 8k hectares, so nothing is inflated.

Delivered. 11 slides in 16:9, a PDF with selectable text, and a PPTX where all 134 text boxes stay editable.

In a real project: your numbers, your story and your brand, a deck for investors or for sales, and updates before every round or pitch.
