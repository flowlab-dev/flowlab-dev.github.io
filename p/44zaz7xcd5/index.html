Services

Restore. Upholster. Trailer. Sell.

  • Premium restorationsFine wooden and classic boats — from a 1904 Fay and Bowen launch to a 1965 Riva Super Ariston.
  • Fine marine leathersFactory colors for Chris Craft, Gar Wood and Hacker Craft, recreated from original samples with our tannery of twenty years.
  • Classic Trail trailersCustom inboard trailers built from scratch around your boat: 16 to 36 feet, surge disc brakes and LED lighting as standard.
  • BrokerageBoats we know firsthand, stored at our warehouse and shown by appointment.
