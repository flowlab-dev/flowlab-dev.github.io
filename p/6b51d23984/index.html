Six kinds of models, one standard.

Modeled by people who know where the file goes next: a factory floor, a game engine or a client presentation.

  • 3D product modeling

    Production-ready models for manufacturing, prototyping, eCommerce and marketing, built from your sketches, reference photos or existing product line.

  • Architectural 3D visualization

    Interior and exterior renderings, floor plans and full walkthroughs for architects, developers and real estate teams, with accurate lighting, material and scale.

  • CAD modeling and drafting

    Sketches, blueprints, PDFs and technical drawings converted into accurate CAD models and complete engineering documentation.

  • 3D game asset modeling

    Characters, props, environments and mechanical assets with clean topology and sensible UVs that work directly in Unreal, Unity or your engine.

  • 3D animation

    Product demonstrations, assembly walkthroughs, architectural fly-throughs and industrial process animations.

  • BIM modeling

    Building Information Models for architects, engineers and contractors, built to support coordination and construction, not just visualization.
