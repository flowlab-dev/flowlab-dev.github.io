How a custom build works

From your measurements to your bike.

  • StyleTell us how you ride: racing, gravel, touring, commuting.
  • DepositYour build takes its place in the queue.
  • PaintStock or custom paint, your choice of decals.
  • BuildHand crafted in Seattle, one at a time, to ISO standards.
  • RideServiced by the same shop that built it.
