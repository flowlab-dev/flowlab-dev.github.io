FAQ

Can the price go up during construction?

No, as long as the plan stays the same: the price of each stage is written into the contract. If you want different windows or roofing, we’ll recalculate and sign an addendum before the work starts.

Can I build with a mortgage?

Yes. Since March 1, 2025, in Russia a mortgage for building a private house under a construction contract goes through an escrow account: the bank’s money sits in the account and is released to the contractor stage by stage or after the house is registered. We work under these contracts and prepare the documents for the bank.

What about a building permit?

For individual housing construction (IZhS), you file a notice of planned construction, and once the house is built, a notice of completion, which is used to register the house. We’ll help you prepare both and file them through Gosuslugi, the Russian public services portal.

What warranty do you give?

10 years on the foundation and walls, 5 years on the roof, windows and utilities. The terms are written into the contract.
