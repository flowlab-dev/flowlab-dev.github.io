Every missed call gets an answer.

For a shop that can't pick up while it's on a job: each missed call or voicemail becomes a lead in Google Sheets, gets up to three short emails from the owner, stops as soon as the customer replies (the inbox is checked every 5 minutes) — and lands on a Monday list. Built on n8n, Gmail and Google Sheets.

Six workflows, ready to import

n8n → Workflows → Import from file. Google Voice sends its notifications to the Gmail you connect here, and the follow-ups go out from that same Gmail. Add Gmail and Google Sheets credentials, paste the sheet ID, fill in Shop settings (name, hours, time zone, email days), and pick workflow 0 as the Error workflow of the others. The schedule lives in the sheet, not in Wait nodes — a restart loses nothing.

  • CaptureReads Google Voice emails, one row per phone number, email from the voicemail.
  • Follow-upsEvery 15 min, shop hours only: emails on day 0, 2 and 5.
  • Stop on replyReply → stop. "unsubscribe" → never again.
  • Monday digestThe owner's list: replies and who to call back.
  • Owner buttonsApprove or stop a lead from the email: random token per lead, confirm page first.
  • Error alertSet it as the Error workflow of 1–5: any failed run emails the owner a link.

What it can't do, honestly

  • Google Voice has no public API.The system reads the email notifications Google Voice sends for missed calls and voicemail. Turn them on once in Google Voice settings.
  • No email, no emails.Most callers leave a number, not an address. Those go to the "call back" list; the system never guesses an address.
  • Transcripts can be wrong.An address spoken in a voicemail is used when it has the shape of a real address — a misheard letter would still get the email. One setting (useSpokenEmail) turns spoken addresses off; then those callers go to the call-back list.
  • Checked on sample emails.The notification parser was tested on sample Google Voice emails. On a real shop account it is confirmed on day one with a test call before anything is sent.
  • Texts need their own setup.SMS follow-ups are possible (for example through Twilio) after US carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) — a separate step.
