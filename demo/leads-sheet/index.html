At night and in the morning

At 3:00 the script moves repeat leads to “Duplicates”, noting the row they repeat. At 9:00 it mails the owner yesterday's leads by source.

What the code does

Any form, one sheet

A website, Tilda or n8n send the lead with a secret key, and a linked Google Form works too. Fields are matched by name, in English or Russian.

Leads don't get lost

Telegram gets three tries. If it is still down, the lead stays in the sheet and the Log says which alert did not go out.

Clean data

Phone numbers come to one format, a hidden field stops bots, and text like =FORMULA() can't run in a cell.

Nothing is deleted

Repeat leads move to their own sheet with the row they repeat, so the owner can move them back.
