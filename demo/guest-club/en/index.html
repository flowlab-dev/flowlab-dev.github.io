One guest, two booking systems, one guest account.

Pine Shore runs city apartments and country houses, and each keeps its bookings in its own system. The club recognizes a guest by phone or email, however the front desk typed them in. It awards bonuses only for completed stays and never twice, however many times a system sends the same booking.

Club rules

The tier depends on the total of completed stays over the last 12 months. The bonus rate is locked in when bonuses are awarded and can only go up if the club later learns about an earlier stay. A bonus payment is locked in when the guest pays. Change the numbers and the demo replays the loaded exports under the new rules, so you can see the difference. In a live club, new rules apply from the day they change, and bonuses already awarded are not recalculated.

Rule checks

These are the rules that decide a guest’s bonuses. They are checked on the same exports and on a few short cases of one to three bookings, right on this page, in a fraction of a second.

    How it would work for you

    The club’s own database

    A separate service with its own database of guests, bookings and the bonus ledger. Its API is documented, so an app, a bot or a CRM can connect to it later.

    Sync over an API

    The booking systems report new and changed bookings themselves, with the time of each change. Before a guest pays with bonuses, they ask the club how much can be used and apply the discount for the guest’s tier. Here the sync is shown with exports; the rules are the same.

    Sign-in with a code

    The guest signs in with a code sent by SMS or a link sent by email. A “Guest account” button appears on both websites.

    Rules without a developer

    The admin changes tier thresholds, rates, the welcome bonus and promo codes. New rules apply from the day they change.

    Tricky cases go to a person

    A phone shared by people with different names, a booking with no contacts, a bonus payment above the limit, a bonus debt. The club puts such cases on a list for the admin.

    Monthly support

    Checking bonuses against bookings, reviewing flagged cards, new offers and tiers, and a report on how many guests went on to book the other location.
