Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start logging; add your hourly rate whenever you like. After you buy Pro, Gumroad shows your license key on the receipt page and emails it to you. Paste it into the app to unlock Pro.

Where are my shifts stored?

On your device only. We do not run a server that stores your hours. Add the app to your home screen so Safari does not clear its data, and export a CSV from time to time as a backup.

How do I cancel?

Use the “Manage membership” link in your Gumroad receipt email, or “Manage or cancel subscription” in the app’s Settings after you enter your key. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Lifetime is a one-time payment with nothing to cancel.

What if my hourly rate changes?

Set the new rate and the date it starts. Earlier pay periods keep the rate you had then; nothing is recalculated backwards.

I travel for work. Will my hours shift?

No. Pick your work time zone once, and trips to other time zones do not move shifts in past weeks.

Which overtime rules does it follow?

Weekly overtime after 40 hours at 1.5×, as in the federal FLSA. With Pro, daily overtime: 1.5× after 8 hours a day, or the California setting with 2× after 12 hours. The seventh-day rule and other special state rules are not calculated. It is a tracking tool, not legal or payroll advice.
